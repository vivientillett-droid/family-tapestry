The Family Tapestry

Use the arrow keys to move between people: up to their parents, down to their children, left and right along the same generation. Press Enter to read about the selected person. Hold Shift with the arrow keys to pan, plus and minus to zoom, H to return home, 0 to see the whole tree. Or use the search box to find someone by name.

How to explore

For a step-by-step guide, press Show me how (the walking person) at the top of the page.

On a phone or tablet

  • Drag with one finger to wander; flick to glide.
  • Pinch to zoom in and out.
  • Double-tap to zoom in; tap with two fingers to zoom out.
  • Tap anyone to meet them. Swipe the details card down to close it, or up to read more.

With a keyboard

  • Tab to the tree, then ↑ parents, ↓ children, ← → along a generation.
  • Enter opens their details, Esc closes.
  • Shift + arrows to pan, + − to zoom, H home, 0 whole tree.
